VFX STUDIO · DIGITAL PRESENCE · GROWTH

Blue Magic VFX — a studio's craft, finally matched online

Blue Magic VFX had the creative chops of a serious studio, but its website and search presence lagged well behind the quality of the work itself — a mismatch that cost it credibility before a prospect ever saw a reel.

1 Creative direction and visual design support across the studio's presentation.
2 Rebuilt the website into a more premium, cinematic digital experience.
3 Layered in SEO marketing built for organic search visibility and growth.
What changed

A digital presence that finally matches the caliber of the studio's actual work — cinematic, credible, and easier to find in search.

EQUIPMENT SALES & SERVICE · BRAND IDENTITY

Sri Adithya Dent — one identity, built to travel across every touchpoint

Sri Adithya Dent had no consistent visual identity — the business looked different on a business card, a uniform and a delivery bag, with nothing tying it together as one premium brand.

1 Designed a refined "SA" monogram as the primary logo system.
2 Built a disciplined color system — deep navy, gold, green and light neutral tones.
3 Rolled the system out across letterhead, business cards, apparel, packaging and stationery.
What changed

One consistent, premium identity system — carried through every printed and physical touchpoint the business owns.

META ADS · LEAD GENERATION · CONVERSION

Techno Foundation — a connected system from ad to enrolment

Techno Foundation was running ads that reached people, but interest was leaking out at every stage after the click — with no connected path from a scroll-stopping ad to a qualified opportunity.

1 Built targeted Meta Ads to reach the right audience.
2 Developed click-worthy creative that earned the scroll-stop, not just the impression.
3 Connected landing page and chat flow to turn interest into an enquiry, and enquiry into a qualified opportunity.
What changed

One connected system — ad, creative, landing and conversion — turning attention into qualified enquiries instead of losing it along the way.
